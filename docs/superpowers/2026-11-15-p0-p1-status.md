# P0/P1 status — 2026-11-15

## What is complete

- **P0 forensic audit: COMPLETE.** Actual local Pendulum checkpoints are
  `KoopmanLitModule`, with audited dimensions, data/checkpoint hashes and
  effective hyperparameters. The legacy YAML recorded lambda_control=0.01 while
  the model used 0.005; locked reruns reproduce the old result. That reporting
  drift is not the cause of the measured historical gap. It does not establish
  that all objective/optimizer choices are harmless.
- **Original CartPole 5% runs: COMPLETE**, seeds 42/43/44, 100 epochs, with
  original results preserved separately from the new causal baselines.
- **P1 causal experiment batch: COMPLETE**, 28 runs × 100 epochs, 353,800 updates,
  Pendulum/CartPole at 5%. Every run has a manifest, history, hashed checkpoint
  and final result. Final paired test comparisons cover seeds 42/43/44 and
  the same 1,000 held-out trajectories. Geometry/forward gradients, invertibility,
  output rank, split separation and independent float64 Gramian checks passed.

## What is not complete

- **Exact paper reproduction: NOT_CERTIFIED / NEEDS_INVESTIGATION.** This is not
  the full Table 1 fraction grid. There are other locally generated CartPole
  C01/C03/C04 artifacts, but they were not part of this 28-run audit and are not
  promoted or included in its causal conclusions.
- The paper explicitly specifies dataset counts, dt, H=15, batch 64, LR 0.001,
  dimensions 16/25 and a 3×64/ReLU encoder. These settings are known and used.
  What remains insufficiently pinned is the actual Table 1 evaluation-window/
  normalization/reduction contract and experimental transform/Gramian settings.
- The original CartPole dataset audit still FAILS at tol=1e-4. The one failing
  transition is exactly replayed by the original float32 solver and is absent
  from the original C02 training subsets. Its numerical explanation is saved;
  legacy generation seeds are not thereby recovered.
- Lower short-horizon MSE does not certify long-horizon fidelity. Removing
  encoder penalties improves Pendulum H=15 but worsens H=250. CartPole start0
  sampling achieves a low score but intentionally changes the paper's stated
  random-window training usage.

## Verified results and interpretation

| Paired 5% experiment | Baseline test H=15 | Variant test H=15 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Pendulum: remove both encoder penalties | 0.08619425 ± 0.04338556 | 0.01447501 ± 0.01196399 |
| CartPole: negate physical transform block, keep random windows | 0.09179632 ± 0.00259836 | 0.06004065 ± 0.00180977 |

Mean ± sample std across three seeds. Every paired seed improves. The CartPole
negative-head + first-window-only diagnostic reaches 0.00087518 ± 0.00012415,
but is **not faithful-paper-reproduction evidence**. The sampler intervention
changes state coverage and unique-window diversity together.

The executed softplus Gramian regularizer differs from the paper formula, but
removing it barely changes seed-42 accuracy in either environment. The evidence
therefore supports several mechanisms, not one universal config repair.
